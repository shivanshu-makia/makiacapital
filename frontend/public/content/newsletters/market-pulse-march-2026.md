## Executive Summary

March 2026 was the worst month for Indian equities in FY26. The Nifty 50 fell 11.3% — its sharpest monthly decline since Feb '22. Brent crude surged past $110/bbl on US–Iran escalation, the rupee hit near-record lows (USD/INR futures: 96.72 on Mar 30), and FII net outflows reached a FY26 high. DII inflows of ₹1,28,066 Cr provided a structural floor but could not prevent an 11% index decline. No sector offered shelter — PSU Banks reversed Feb's entire 15.3% rally in a single month.

## Sectoral Performance

**PSU Bank (–20.0%):** Reversed Feb's entire rally in one month. SBI (51% index weight) fell 3.9% on Mar 27 alone — FII concentrated selling, NRI deposit outflow risk, and rising bond yields combined to erase 12 months of gains.

**Pharma (–0.8%):** The relative outperformer — held up by USD earnings translation benefit, US generics demand stability, and defensive rotation. ONGC (+4.03%) and Coal India (+0.32%) were the only Nifty 50 stocks with meaningful positive closes.

## Global & Geopolitical Developments — March 2026

### 01 — US–Iran Standoff: Gulf on War Footing
Russia struck Ukraine with 948 drones. The Strait of Hormuz (20% of global oil supply) remained largely closed to commercial shipping.

**India Impact:** Prolonged disruption structurally pressures India's CAD, OMC margins, and RBI's inflation tolerance.

### 02 — Brent >$110/bbl: Singapore GRMs Collapse
Crude surged on supply fears. Singapore GRMs fell from ~$44/bbl to negative — direct margin shock for BPCL and HPCL.

**India Impact:** ONGC (+4.03%) and Coal India (+0.32%) were the only Nifty gainers on Mar 30. Aviation faces structural cost pressure.

### 03 — Gold Falls 20% from Peak
Gold fell nearly 20% from its recent high on forced liquidation as investors sold gold to meet equity margin calls.

**India Impact:** MCX gold's correction reduces near-term hedging value for equity-market participants who had rotated into gold.

### 04 — ECB: Lagarde Says Damage May Last 'Years'
ECB President Lagarde said the bank was prepared to change policy at any meeting and that the Iran war disruption 'could last years.'

**India Impact:** ECB hawkishness widens global yield differentials, tightens EM dollar liquidity, accelerates FII outflows.

### 05 — TotalEnergies Exits US Wind; Pivots to Fossil Fuels
TotalEnergies agreed to renounce offshore-wind US licences and invest $928m in a Texas LNG plant.

**India Impact:** Reduces global capital competition for Indian renewable projects — marginally positive for India's clean-energy capex cycle.

### 06 — ARM Unveils AGI CPU
ARM (SoftBank-controlled) launched its first AI data-centre chip. Lead customers: Meta and OpenAI.

**India Impact:** Reinforces AI-driven EM rotation to Korea (+22% YTD) and Taiwan (+10% YTD). India's MSCI EM weight: sub-14% vs 21% in 2024.

### 07 — OpenAI Cancels $1bn Disney Deal
OpenAI cancelled Sora's $1bn Disney deal for AI video generation using Disney characters.

**India Impact:** Tightening AI content regulation is a medium-term positive for India's sovereign AI model initiative.

### 08 — Australia–EU FTA Signed; Mercosur from May 1
After 8 years, Australia and EU signed a free-trade pact. EU-Mercosur pact takes provisional effect May 1.

**India Impact:** Increases competitive pressure on Indian exporters; accelerates urgency of India-EU and India-UK FTA negotiations.

### 09 — India–Israel: Defence JV + 50,000 Worker Visas
PM Modi confirmed joint defence development, technology transfer. Israel agreed to 50,000 additional Indian worker visas.

**India Impact:** Positive for domestic defence manufacturers. FTA conclusion expands high-value export market access.

### 10 — IMF Approves ₹74,700 Cr Ukraine Loan
The IMF approved a 4-year loan for Ukraine. Ukraine faces a ₹4.8 lakh Cr financing gap in 2026.

**India Impact:** Prolonged conflict sustains wheat and sunflower oil supply chain stress — direct input to India's food CPI basket.

### 11 — Danish Election: Greenland Features in Campaign
Denmark's indecisive election triggered coalition talks. Greenland featured as a campaign issue amid US interest.

**India Impact:** Arctic resource politics and NATO cohesion affect long-term global energy supply diversification timelines.

### 12 — US Midterm: Democrats Win Trump's Florida District
Democrats won a special election in a Republican +19pt district — the one containing Trump's Mar-a-Lago estate.

**India Impact:** A US Congressional shift by end-2026 could affect Iran sanctions, trade policy, and energy transition pace.
